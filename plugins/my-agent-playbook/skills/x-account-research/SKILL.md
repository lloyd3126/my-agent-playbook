# X Account Research

Read [references/workflow.md](references/workflow.md) completely, then follow its scope, evidence, date, and knowledge-file rules.

Treat the workflow as read-only. Establish the account identity and time window, gather current public primary posts when available, retain links and dates, distinguish direct claims from inference, and update only the requested knowledge record. Do not interact with the account or publish anything.

If login is required for visibility, let the user authenticate directly; never request credentials, cookies, tokens, or two-factor codes in chat.
